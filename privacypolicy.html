PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Binality is a free, open-source brainwave entrainment (binaural beats) app and website published by Alexis Matthew Tivey under the name Virtual Arenas. This policy covers the Binality website at https://binality.virtualarenas.com/ and the Binality apps for Android and iOS.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.
  • Your settings stay on your device. We never receive them.

Information we collect

None. Binality generates its tones on your device. It does not send any information about you or your use of it to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that receives data from the app.

Information stored on your device

Binality remembers your preferences so it opens the way you left it: for example the selected program, tone and ambient volume, base frequency, timer setting, display options, and any custom presets you save. This is kept in your device's local storage (browser localStorage on the website; the same storage plus the operating system's native preferences store in the apps). It is never transmitted. You can remove it by clearing the site data for Binality in your browser, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

Network use

The Binality apps make no network requests of their own and work fully offline; the fonts and audio engine are bundled with the app.

The website is different because it is a web page. Your browser downloads its files from our web host, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time). We do not use those logs to identify or profile you. The website's fonts are hosted with the site itself, so no request is made to Google or any other third party to display it. The website can be installed as a web app and caches its files in your browser so it works offline. It does not use cookies.

Android: notification and permissions

To keep audio playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background, the Android app runs a foreground service and shows an ongoing notification with a Stop control. It uses these permissions:

  • Notifications: to show that notification. It contains no personal data and is never sent anywhere.
  • Wake lock: to keep the device awake enough for audio to continue during a session. It is released when playback stops.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to allow audio to continue in the background.

Binality does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or storage.

Links to other sites

Binality links to the project's GitHub page (https://github.com/amtivey/binality) and to a Wikipedia article about binaural beats (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binaural_beats). These open in your browser and are operated by third parties, whose privacy practices are their own.

Third parties

We do not share data with anyone because we do not collect any. Binality contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software.

Children's privacy

Binality is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect any from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us.

Health and safety

  • Binaural beats need stereo headphones to work as intended.
  • Keep the volume low. Prolonged loud listening can damage your hearing.
  • Do not use Binality while driving or operating machinery, or in any situation where you need to stay alert to your surroundings.
  • Binality is for relaxation and entertainment only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Program names such as "Pain Relief" and "Universal Healing" describe traditional uses of binaural beats, not claims of medical benefit.
  • If you have epilepsy, a heart condition or any other medical concern, consult a doctor before use. Stop if you feel unwell.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. If Binality ever changes in a way that collects data, this policy will be updated before that change is released.
